Summary – CFO supply plan to Kiunga, 19 Nov 2026 to 13 Jan 2027

Prepared by Myles Rowcliff, OKTL.

The plan keeps Tabubil above one day of use every day and delivers about 37.9 ML to Kiunga over 8 weeks (10.70 ML by river, 27.20 ML by truck), against 19.6 ML of site use.

  • Site use: 350,000 L a day.
  • Trucks: the 7 trucks already loaded (280,000 L) and 4 pre-filled diesel tank-tainers (4 × 21,000 L = 84,000 L, not from Amigo 1) left Aiambak on the morning of 19 Nov. The road is closed, so no convoys ran on 20 Nov; the plan assumes it reopens at 6 AM 21 Nov (to be confirmed). The 12 trucks filled on the evening of 19 Nov wait at Aiambak and go first when it reopens. Then 14 trucks a day (560,000 L) in three convoys: A at 5 AM (6 trucks), B at 6 AM (4 trucks), C at 7 AM (4 trucks). Convoys leave on time; any truck that can't be fuelled waits at Aiambak for the next convoy.
  • Fly Challenger – one-off run to Kiunga: left Aiambak at 8 AM 19 Nov with 1.2 ML, due at Kiunga about 8 AM 22 Nov. After 12 hours discharging it returns to Aiambak (25 Nov) and on to POM (about 8 PM 29 Nov), then goes back to normal POM – Aiambak trips.
  • Fly Resilience – one-off run to Kiunga: left Aiambak at 8:30 AM 20 Nov with all 1.5 ML (none went into Amigo 1), due at Kiunga about 8:30 AM 23 Nov. After 12 hours discharging it returns to Aiambak (about 26 Nov) and on to POM (about 8:30 PM 30 Nov), then normal POM – Aiambak trips. Assumes the same river times as Challenger (3 days each way).
  • Amigo 1: 1,650,000 L on the evening of 19 Nov (capacity 2,100,000 L). 12 trucks (480,000 L) were filled from it that evening, leaving 1,170,000 L.
  • Fly vessels: Challenger, Resilience and Prosperity at 1.5 ML from their next loading, Warrior 1.0 ML; Warrior starts loading at POM on 21 Nov (taken as midday) and reaches Aiambak about midday 26 Nov.
  • Diamond: POM – Aiambak shuttle (1 day loading, 4 days each way, 12 hours discharging). Delayed at POM: now starts loading at midday 22 Nov, reaches Aiambak about midday 27 Nov and discharges all 950,000 L into Amigo 1 – arriving with Fly Prosperity, so it waits about 30 hours for room and leaves about 6 AM 29 Nov. After that it refuels Elimbari when it is waiting, otherwise it discharges into Amigo 1.
  • Elimbari – one-off trip to POM: delayed – now reaches Kiunga about 3 PM 20 Nov and discharges its 1.0 ML overnight (finishing about 3 AM 21 Nov). It is back at Aiambak empty about 3 PM 23 Nov. Instead of waiting there for CFO, it sails to POM (about 3 PM 27 Nov), loads 1.0 ML and goes straight to Kiunga, arriving about 3 AM 5 Dec.
  • Elimbari – from 8 Dec: back on normal Aiambak – Kiunga runs, about one full run (950,000 – 1,000,000 L) a week. It gets first call on Fly vessel and Diamond CFO at Aiambak.
  • Wonderland: POM – Aiambak shuttle into Amigo 1 from 5 Dec, 9-day cycle with 1.5 days at each end (1 day freight, 12 hours CFO).
  • Site stock: with no trucks on 20 Nov and Elimbari arriving late, stock runs down to about 554,000 L (1.6 days of use) at the end of 20 Nov and about 524,000 L (1.5 days) around 1 AM 21 Nov – the lowest point in the plan. The plan counts Elimbari's 1.0 ML in full when it finishes discharging (about 3 AM 21 Nov), so in practice some of it will be on site before midnight. Trucks restart on 21 Nov, Challenger discharges 1.2 ML from 22 Nov and Resilience 1.5 ML from 23 Nov; from 22 Nov stock stays above 3 ML. There is then no river delivery until Elimbari arrives from POM on 5 Dec; trucks carry the full load in between.

Trucks

Convoys leave on time with every truck Amigo 1 can fill; a truck that can't be fuelled waits at Aiambak for the next convoy. The daily target stays at 14 trucks (one tanker is down for maintenance; the plan isn't changed for it). With Fly Resilience's 1.5 ML going straight to Kiunga instead of into Amigo 1, Amigo 1 has only 1,170,000 L to work with until Fly Warrior arrives about midday 26 Nov. Short days: 20 Nov – none, road closed; 23 Nov – 13 trucks (520,000 L); 24 – 26 Nov – no trucks, Amigo 1 empty; 3 Dec – 2 trucks; 4 Dec – none until Fly Challenger arrives that evening. Site stock is at least 3.7 ML on those days, so Tabubil isn't affected.

Risks and open items

  • Road closure and site stock: the plan assumes the road reopens at 6 AM 21 Nov. Site stock is at its lowest tonight (about 1.5 days of use). If the road stays closed on 21 Nov, stock would be about 1.2 ML at the end of 21 Nov (Elimbari's fuel only) and Challenger's 1.2 ML arrives on 22 Nov, so Tabubil stays above one day of use, but each extra day closed means another 560,000 L not trucked.
  • Road, 23 Nov – 5 Dec: while Elimbari is on its trip to POM, Kiunga gets CFO by road only (after Resilience on 23 Nov). If the road closed in that window, nothing would reach Kiunga until Elimbari arrives on 5 Dec. Site stock at the start of the window is about 3.2 ML (about 9 days of use).
  • Road, after 5 Dec: if the road closes, Kiunga relies on Elimbari alone, about 1 ML a week – well under site use over a long closure.
  • Elimbari to POM: assumes Elimbari takes the same times as a Fly vessel between Aiambak and POM (4 days each way) and that CFO is ready for it at POM with 1 day loading.
  • Fly vessel waiting: about 287 hours in total over 8 weeks while Amigo 1 is full – mostly in early December, including about 84 hours for Diamond's second trip (8 – 12 Dec) and about 30 hours for its first (27 – 29 Nov).
How the schedule is worked out

POM–Aiambak round trip 9 days (including a day alongside at Aiambak) plus 1 day loading at POM; a Fly vessel does not leave Aiambak until all its CFO is off, so it waits when Amigo 1 is full. Aiambak–Kiunga round trip 5 days plus 12 hours refuelling at Aiambak and 12 hours discharging at Kiunga. River vessels get first call on Fly vessel CFO at Aiambak; whatever is left goes into Amigo 1, which trucks out in three convoys a day. Wonderland shuttles POM–Aiambak on a 9-day cycle into Amigo 1. Diamond (950,000 L) shuttles POM–Aiambak like a Fly vessel. The schedule is simulated hour by hour. Lead time counts only CFO that goes to Kiunga by river.
